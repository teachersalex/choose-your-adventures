Don’t Read

Read each part. At the end, choose 1 or 2. There are three endings. Can you find the good one?

Part 1

The Call

Your phone rings. It’s late at night.

“Agent, this is Mr. Verity. I have a job for you.”

You are a new agent of the Order. This is your first mission.

“There’s an old library in a small town,” he says. “It’s closed now. Only one man works there: Osvaldo, the night guard. Last night, Osvaldo didn’t go home. His daughter, Ana, is very worried. And at night, people in town see gold lights in the windows of the library.”

“What do I do?” you ask.

“Go there tonight and look around. Don’t fight. A team arrives tomorrow morning. And if you see gold symbols, don’t read them. Sometimes it’s better not to know.”

An hour later, you are in front of the old library. The front door is open. At the back of the building, there’s a small light.

What do you do?

Part 2

The Hall

You go in. It’s very dark, so you turn on your flashlight.

The walls are full of gold symbols. They move slowly, like insects. The Order has a name for them: sigils.

You remember Mr. Verity’s words: don’t read them. You look at the floor.

Then you hear whispers: many quiet voices. They come from two places: from the stairs down to the basement, and from the stairs up to the reading room.

What do you do?

Part 3

The Guard’s Room

You walk to the back of the building. The light comes from a small room with an open door. It’s the guard’s room.

There’s a chair, a radio, and a cup of cold coffee. On the wall, there’s a photo of a man and a young woman. They are smiling. Under the photo, someone wrote: “Me and Ana.” So this man is Osvaldo.

On the table, there’s a notebook. You open it and read:

10 p.m. Everything OK.

11 p.m. A noise in the basement.

11:30 p.m. I found a book. A very old book.

After that, there are no words. There are only gold symbols. On the last page, the symbols move.

What do you do?

Part 4

The Stairs

You go down the stairs to the basement. The stairs are old, and every step makes a noise. It’s very cold here.

At the bottom of the stairs, there’s a door with a small sign: ARCHIVE.

Under the door, you see a gold light. Behind the door, a man whispers. You can’t understand his words.

You look at your phone. There’s a signal, but it’s weak.

Part 5

The Reading Room

You go up the stairs. The reading room is big and dark. There are books on the floor, hundreds of books. All of them are open, and every page is full of gold symbols.

The whispers are very loud here. You listen, and you hear one word again and again.

It’s your name.

You are very afraid. Your hands shake. Maybe Osvaldo is in this building. Maybe he needs help. But the front door is very close.

What do you do?

Part 6

The Archive

You open the door. The room is small and full of old boxes.

In the middle of the room, a man sits at a table. It’s the night guard, Osvaldo. He has a big old book in his hands. The gold light comes from the pages of the book.

Osvaldo reads and reads. He whispers the words. His eyes are white. There are gold symbols on his hands and on his face.

He doesn’t look at you. He doesn’t stop.

What do you do?

Part 7

Mr. Verity

Mr. Verity answers. “Tell me, agent.”

You tell him everything: the gold light, the whispers, the man behind the door.

For a moment, he is quiet. Then he says: “Gold symbols. Whispers. This is the element of Knowledge. Knowledge is calm. It has no feelings. Blood is different. Blood is the element of strong feelings: love, pain, hate. And Blood is stronger than Knowledge. Remember that.”

“Can the team come now?” you ask.

“No. The team is far away. And Osvaldo can’t wait until morning. It’s your decision, agent.”

Then the call ends. No signal.

What do you do?

Part 8

The Book

You take the book with both hands and pull. But Osvaldo’s hands are strong. You pull again, harder.

The book falls on the floor. It opens. Osvaldo doesn’t move. He looks at the open book and whispers.

Gold light fills the room. Now the whispers are inside your head. “Read,” they say. “Read. Read.”

You look down at the page. The symbols are strange, but you almost understand them. Maybe you can understand everything.

What do you do?

Part 9

Osvaldo

You don’t look at the book. You look at Osvaldo.

“Osvaldo,” you say. “Can you hear me?”

He doesn’t answer. He only whispers the words of the book. His face is very calm. Too calm. He isn’t happy. He isn’t sad. He isn’t afraid. There is nothing in his face.

You need to wake him up. But how?

The gold light is warm and beautiful. Maybe the answer is in the book.

What do you do?

Part 10

The Words

You read.

At first, the symbols are strange. Then, slowly, they are not strange. You understand one. Then ten. Then a hundred.

The whispers are happy now. They tell you everything about the Other Side, the world of the paranormal. You want more, so you read more.

You are not afraid now. You are not sad. You feel nothing.

You forget the library. You forget the Order. Then you forget your name.

In the morning, the Order finds you. You are sitting on the floor, reading. You don’t stop. You don’t want to stop.

    Part 11

    The Empty Library

    You leave the library. You sit in your car and wait. You don’t sleep.

    In the morning, the team from the Order arrives. You go into the library with them.

    The walls are clean. There are no symbols and no whispers. The team looks everywhere. In the basement, in a small room, there’s a table and a chair. Nothing more. No Osvaldo.

    You are alive. But you are very sad. You didn’t help Osvaldo.

    The Order looks for him for months, but they never find him. And at night, people in town still see gold lights. Now they are in other windows.

      Part 12

      Ana

      “Osvaldo, listen to me. Your daughter is waiting for you. She is very worried. She needs you.”

      He whispers. He doesn’t stop.

      You speak louder. “Ana loves you! Go home to Ana!”

      Osvaldo stops whispering. Slowly, his face changes. First, he is confused. Then he is afraid. Then he starts to cry.

      His eyes are not white now. One by one, the gold symbols disappear from his skin.

      “Ana,” he says. “Where is Ana?”

      You close the book. You don’t look at the pages. The whispers stop, and the gold light goes out.

      That night, you take Osvaldo home. Ana opens the door. She hugs her father, and she cries too.

      In the morning, the team from the Order arrives and takes the book.

      Your phone rings. It’s Mr. Verity.

      “Good work, agent.”

      THE END. MISSION COMPLETE.
