Don’t Close the Book

This book belongs to:

Last week, you fell into your old princess book. This morning, the princesses are in your bed. Read each part. At the end, choose 1 or 2. Your choice changes everything. There are six endings. Find them all.

Part 1

Saturday Morning

Saturday, 8 a.m. You open your eyes. There is a foot on your face: Merida’s foot. Rapunzel is hugging you. Aurora is asleep on your computer. On the screen: kkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkkk.

Ten princesses are in your apartment. Snow White is washing your dishes with help from the pigeons. Cinderella is cleaning your bathroom. Ariel is combing her hair with your fork. Moana is in the shower, talking to the water.

On the sofa, there is one more woman, with long brown hair and a shell necklace. She is singing, and her voice is beautiful. “Hi! I’m Vanessa, a princess from a new story.”

Ariel looks at her and opens her mouth. No words come.

Your old princess book is open on the floor. The pages are white, but on the first page, someone wrote in red:

THE DOOR OPENS AT MIDNIGHT.

DON’T CLOSE THE BOOK.

DON’T GIVE IT TO ANYONE.

“My fairy godmother wrote this!” says Cinderella.

There are two eggs in your fridge, and eleven hungry princesses. “I can go to the bakery with you,” says Vanessa. Ariel holds your arm and shakes her head.

What do you do?

Part 2

The Elevator

You and Vanessa get in the elevator. There is a big mirror on the wall, and small letters say: CAPACITY: 8 PEOPLE.

You look in the mirror, and you are very afraid. Next to you, there is no young woman. There is a big old woman with purple skin, white hair and long black arms, like an octopus.

You look at Vanessa. She is beautiful. You look at the mirror again. Now she is beautiful there too.

“Are you OK, dear?” she asks. She smiles, and her shell necklace shines.

At the bakery, you buy forty bread rolls. Then you are in the elevator again, and Vanessa is very close to you. Your hands shake.

What do you do?

Part 3

The Bathroom Mirror

Vanessa goes to the bakery alone. You sit with Ariel. “What’s wrong?” you ask. She can’t answer.

After Moana’s hot shower, the bathroom mirror is all white. Ariel takes your hand and runs to it. With her finger, she draws on the glass: a shell, a mouth, and a woman with many long arms.

Belle looks at the drawing. “Your voice is in a shell?” Ariel says yes with her head. Then she points at the sofa, where Vanessa sat.

Moana looks at the drawing too. “A woman with many arms. She is from the sea.” She takes Ariel’s hand. “The ocean is my friend. Maybe it knows your family. Let’s fill the bathtub.”

Part 4

Bread and a Party

Ten o’clock. There is bread on the table, and there are eleven princesses in your small kitchen. Vanessa makes coffee and sings. When she sings, her shell necklace shines, and Ariel puts a hand on her throat.

Then Vanessa sees the book on the floor and picks it up. “Ouch!” She drops it fast. “It’s hot!” Cinderella looks at her. “The book doesn’t like you,” she says.

Your phone rings. It’s your best friend, Julia. “My birthday party is tonight at nine! My birthday is tomorrow, so at midnight, we sing Happy Birthday!”

Jasmine runs to you. “A party in the city? Please! In my story, I never leave the palace.”

Vanessa claps her hands. “Yes! A party! Take us all!”

Part 5

The Contract

“Who are you?” you ask.

Vanessa presses STOP, and the elevator stops between two floors. She laughs, and it is not a young woman’s laugh.

“I’m Ursula, dear, the sea witch. Last night, I took the princesses out of the book. At midnight, the door opens, and they can go home. But I want the book closed, with them outside. Then every story is mine.”

“Why don’t you close it?” you ask.

“I can’t. It burns my hands. But you can give it to me.” She takes a gold paper from her bag.

The princesses go home today.

Your name: ______________

At the bottom, there are some very small letters. You can’t read them.

“Sign here,” says Ursula, “and they go home now. No waiting. No problems.”

Part 6

The Bathtub

Moana puts her hands in the water and talks to it very quietly. The water moves. Then a face appears in it: an old man with a long white beard and a gold crown.

Ariel cries and puts her hands in the water too. It is her father, King Triton.

“My daughter! Where is your voice?” Then he understands. “The sea witch! Ursula! She can look like a beautiful young woman, and she keeps your voice in a shell.”

He looks at you. “At midnight, the door in the book opens. Then I can come through this water. Keep the book open until midnight. And don’t trust that woman.”

Then the water is quiet again.

Part 7

The Club

At nine, you take ten princesses and Vanessa to Julia’s party, in a club in the city. Mulan carries Aurora. Aurora is still asleep.

The music is loud, and the lights are pink and blue. Jasmine dances on a table. “I love your world!” Your friends love the princesses. Everybody takes photos and videos. “Are they actresses?” asks Julia.

At 11:30, Cinderella runs to you. “Midnight is very close, and the book is at home! We need to go now!”

Vanessa takes your hand. She smiles. “No, no, dear. One more song. Midnight is not important.”

Vanessa sits next to you. “Go to your party, dear. Give me the book. I can stay with it all night. I love books.”

Part 9

Five Minutes to Midnight

All day, the book is in your bathroom, under a towel. All day, Vanessa looks for it, and the princesses stay close to you.

At 11:55, she finds it. But she can’t touch it, because it burns her hands. So she takes Ariel by the arm. She doesn’t smile now.

“Give me the book,” she says, “or this little mermaid comes with me, under the sea. Forever.”

Ariel cries. She shakes her head: no, no, no.

Part 10

Capacity: 8 People

You run to your building with the princesses and Vanessa. On the steps, Cinderella loses a glass shoe. “Not again!”

Everyone gets in the elevator: you, ten princesses and Vanessa. The elevator is for eight people. You are twelve. It goes up very slowly, and then it stops between two floors.

In the big mirror, everybody can see Vanessa’s true face: purple skin, white hair and long black arms. “A mirror always tells the truth,” says Snow White. “In my story too.”

Vanessa laughs. “Yes. I’m Ursula, the sea witch.” She takes a gold paper from her bag. “Sign this, and the elevator moves. Don’t sign it, and you are all here at midnight.”

It’s 11:57.

Part 11

Happy Birthday

At nine, Julia and fifteen friends arrive with a big cake. Now your small apartment is very, very full. Your friends think the princesses are actresses in costumes. “Your party is amazing!” says Julia.

Rapunzel paints flowers on Julia’s face. Jasmine dances. Aurora sleeps on your computer again.

At 11:55, Vanessa takes your arm. “At midnight, I sing Happy Birthday,” she says, “with my beautiful voice.” Her shell necklace shines, and Ariel puts a hand on her throat. Ariel looks at the shell, then at you.

Then Vanessa gives you a gold paper. “Your friends are here, dear. Ten princesses are a problem. Sign this, and they go home now, very quietly. Then you can enjoy your party.”

Part 12

The Garden

You sign. The gold paper shines, and now you can read some very small letters at the bottom:

I GIVE MY BOOK TO URSULA.

I GIVE MYSELF TO URSULA.

Vanessa laughs, and now she is Ursula: purple skin, white hair, long black arms. “Thank you, dear!”

Then there is water everywhere. You are very small and gray, in a garden at the bottom of the sea. Next to you, there are many small gray plants. They signed too.

Now the book is Ursula’s. At midnight, she closes it. The princesses never go home, and every story in the book has a new ending: Ursula wins.
