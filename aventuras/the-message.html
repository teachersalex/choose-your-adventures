Part 1

Friday Night

Friday night. You are at Diego’s apartment. Tomorrow is your wedding.

After the wedding, you and Diego go to Paris. You pay for everything. The tickets are in your bag.

Diego is in the shower. His phone is on the sofa, next to you. It makes a sound. You look.

Messages

11:46 p.m. Paula: Are you there?

11:47 p.m. Paula: Please don’t marry her. I love you. ❤️

Paula is your best friend. Tomorrow, at the wedding, she stands next to you.

What do you do?

Part 3

Diego

Diego comes out of the bathroom.

“Diego, your phone. Messages from Paula.”

You give him the phone. He reads the messages. He is quiet. Then he smiles.

“Oh, Paula... She is crazy. She likes me, but I don’t like her.”

He kisses you.

“Tomorrow, you are my wife.”

What do you do?

Part 4

The Church

Saturday. The church is full. Two hundred people are there.

Diego is at the altar. He smiles. Paula is next to you. She smiles too.

Then, the big question: “Do you take Diego as your husband?”

You take the microphone. “Before I answer, I have a message for everyone. From Diego to Paula.”

You read it: “She pays for Paris. Then bye bye.”

The church is very quiet. Diego’s face is red. Paula’s face is white.

You give your flowers to Paula. “You want him? He is yours.”

And you walk out of the church. Alone. Free.

        Part 7

        The Wife

        You take your phone and call Paula. You turn on the speaker.

        “Hi, friend!” Paula says.

        “Hi, Paula. Diego is here. He says you are crazy.”

        Silence.

        Then Paula screams: “Crazy?! Diego, tell her the truth! Tell her about your WIFE!”

        “His... wife?” Diego looks at the floor.

        “Her name is Renata,” Paula says. “She lives in Curitiba. They have two kids.”
