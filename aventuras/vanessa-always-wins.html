Part 1

The Party

Saturday afternoon. A big party at the Albuquerque house: a party for Vanessa’s baby.

Vanessa is six months pregnant. The father is Rafael. Next month, they’re getting married. But Rafael doesn’t love Vanessa. He’s marrying her because of the baby.

Five years ago, you were Joana, the maid here, and Rafael was your boyfriend. Then Vanessa put her gold necklace in your bag and called the police. Rafael believed her. Vanessa always wins.

Your mother, Cida, was the cook here. Before she died, she said: “I’m sorry... the box... in the kitchen...”

Today, you are back. You are rich, and you have a new name. Nobody knows you. In your bag, you have a plan:

Step 1: Find Vanessa’s secret.

Step 2: Destroy Vanessa.

Vanessa stands up. Her hand is on her big belly. “I’m tired,” she says. She goes upstairs.

What do you do?

Part 2

The Mirror

You go up the stairs. Vanessa’s door is open a little.

Vanessa is in front of the mirror. Her hand goes under her dress. She takes out a pillow. No belly. No baby.

You take a photo with your phone. She doesn’t see you.

She throws the pillow on the bed and laughs. “Next month, I’m marrying Rafael. Then I cry and say: ’I lost the baby.’ Poor me!”

Downstairs, on a big TV, there are photos of Vanessa and her belly. But up here, Vanessa is alone. Now she is in your hands.

What do you do?

Part 3

The Box

The kitchen is the same. Your mother’s old chair is there.

Behind the coffee, you find a little box. Inside, there is a letter.

My daughter,

You and Vanessa were born on the same night, in the same hospital. I changed the babies.

Vanessa is my daughter. You are Vera’s daughter.

I wanted a good life for my baby. I’m sorry.

Cida

You sit in Cida’s chair. Your hands shake.

Vera Albuquerque. Vanessa’s mother. For Vera, the family name is everything. And she is your mother.

Through the window, you see Vera in the garden, alone.

What do you do?

Part 4

The Big TV

You go downstairs and connect your phone to the big TV. New photo: Vanessa in front of the mirror, with a pillow in her hands.

Silence. Everybody looks at Vanessa. She is on the stairs, with her big belly. Her face is white.

“It’s a lie!” she says.

But Rafael looks at you. “Who are you?”

“You don’t remember me? Joana. The maid. Vanessa put that necklace in my bag, Rafael.”

Rafael’s eyes are wet. “Joana... I’m so sorry. Coffee tomorrow? Just coffee?”

You smile. “Just coffee.”

On the stairs, Vanessa sits down, alone. Today, Vanessa doesn’t win.

    Part 5

    My Baby!

    You go in and show Vanessa the photo. “Remember me? Joana. The maid.”

    Vanessa isn’t afraid. She smiles.

    Very fast, she takes your phone and deletes the photo. She puts the pillow under her dress again. Then she falls on the floor and screams: “HELP! MY BABY!”

    Everybody runs up the stairs. Rafael is the first. Vanessa is crying. “It’s Joana, the maid! She pushed me! My baby!”

    That night, at the hospital, Vanessa says: “I lost the baby.” Her plan, one month early. Rafael holds her hand all night.

    And you? You are at the police station again.

    Five years ago, a necklace. Today, a baby. Vanessa always wins.

      Part 6

      Not My Daughter

      You go to the living room and hit a glass with a spoon. Everybody looks at you: Vera, Vanessa, a hundred people.

      You read the letter to everybody. Silence. Then Vanessa laughs. “Mom, this woman is crazy!”

      Vera takes the letter and reads it. Her face is red.

      “Cida was a liar,” Vera says. She puts the letter in her glass of wine. The words disappear. “Vanessa is my daughter. My only daughter. Security!”

      Two men take your arms. At the door, you look back. Vera is hugging Vanessa. For one second, Vera looks at you. Her eyes are wet. Then she looks away.

      Today, you find your mother. And today, you lose her.

        Part 7

        The Garden

        In the garden, you give Vera the letter. She reads it again and again. She sits down.

        She looks at your face for a long time. “Your eyes... my mother’s eyes.” She cries and hugs you. “My daughter.”

        Then she stands up. “I need to see Vanessa.”

        You go upstairs with her and wait at the door. Vera hugs Vanessa. Her hand is on Vanessa’s belly. Vera’s face changes.

        Back in the garden, Vera says: “Vanessa’s belly is soft. There is no baby.”

        “It’s a pillow,” you say.

        Vera holds your hand. “I know Vanessa. She always wins. Do we tell everybody now? Or do we do a DNA test and wait two weeks?”

        Part 8

        Two Daughters

        At the party, Vera stops the music. Everybody looks at her. Vanessa is next to Rafael, with her big belly.

        “This is Joana, Cida’s daughter. Remember her? But she isn’t Cida’s daughter. She is my daughter.”

        Vanessa laughs. “Mom, what is this?”

        Vera pulls Vanessa’s dress, hard. The pillow falls on the floor. Rafael looks at it for a long time. Then he walks out.

        Two weeks later, the DNA test says yes.

        Vanessa leaves the house with one bag. At the door, you give her the little box. “Your mother’s things. Cida was your mother.”

        That night, you sleep in this house again. Not in the maid’s room. In your room.

          Part 9

          Two Weeks

          You and Vera do the DNA test. You wait.

          But Vanessa knows everything in this house. One night, she finds the DNA papers in Vera’s bag. Two names: Vera Albuquerque and Joana.

          The next morning, Vanessa’s room is empty. On the bed, there is only the pillow.

          At the bank, Vera’s face is white. Vanessa knows the family passwords. The money is gone.

          That afternoon, the DNA result arrives: yes. You are Vera’s daughter.

          In Cida’s old kitchen, Vera makes coffee for you. It’s terrible coffee. You laugh. She laughs too.

          No money. Vanessa always wins. But tonight, you have a mother.
