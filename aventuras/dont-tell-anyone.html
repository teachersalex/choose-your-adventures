Part 1

Three Results

You are sixteen, and you live in Candor. In Candor, people always tell the truth.

Today is the test. A Dauntless woman gives you a small glass of serum. Her name is Tori. In the test, you see strange things: a dog, a knife, a man with a question. And you know: this isn’t real.

You open your eyes. Tori is very worried.

“Candor. Dauntless. Erudite,” she says quietly. “Three results. You are Divergent. It’s dangerous. Don’t tell anyone. Not your family. Not your friends.”

She types CANDOR into the computer.

“In a simulation, Divergents know it isn’t real, and they can change things. Never do it. Someone is always watching.”

Tomorrow is the Choosing Ceremony. You are very afraid.

What do you do?

Part 2

The Train

The Dauntless train comes, very fast. It doesn’t stop. Young Dauntless in black jump off it. They laugh and run. You want to run with them.

Your friend Lia is there too. She lives on your street. “I want to be Dauntless,” she says. “But I’m afraid of everything.”

“What was your test result?” you ask.

Lia looks at the ground. “I can’t talk about it.” Then she says quietly, “My mother says Erudite is looking for people with strange tests.”

Now you are afraid too. And your family is waiting for dinner.

What do you do?

Part 3

Dinner

Tonight there is a guest at dinner: Mr. Vance, an old friend of your father. He is from Erudite. He wears blue, and he asks a lot of questions.

In Candor, every family talks about the day at dinner. Everybody tells the truth.

“So,” says your father. “What was your test result?”

Everybody looks at you. Your little brother smiles. Your mother waits. Mr. Vance puts down his fork. You are very nervous.

“Tell us,” says your father. “In this house, we never lie.”

You remember Tori’s words: don’t tell anyone. But in Candor, a lie is the worst thing.

What do you do?

Part 4

Faction Before Blood

The next morning is the Choosing Ceremony. There are five big bowls on the floor: grey stones for Abnegation, water for Erudite, earth for Amity, glass for Candor and hot coals for Dauntless.

Every sixteen-year-old cuts their hand with a knife. Their blood falls into one bowl, and they can never change.

“Faction before blood,” everybody says.

Your friend Lia goes before you. Her hand shakes. Her blood falls on the hot coals. “Dauntless!” the Dauntless shout.

Then it’s your turn. You cut your hand. Your family watches you from the Candor seats. You are very nervous.

What do you do?

Part 5

The Roof

After the ceremony, the Dauntless run out, and you run with them. They all jump onto a moving train. Then they jump off it, onto a roof. You and Lia jump too.

On the roof, a young Dauntless leader is waiting. His name is Eric. Behind him, there is a big, dark hole in the roof. Nobody can see the bottom.

“This is the way in,” says Eric. “Jump, or you are Factionless. No home. No faction. Nothing.”

One by one, the new Dauntless jump into the dark.

Lia doesn’t move. She is very afraid. “I can’t,” she whispers. “I can’t do it.”

What do you do?

Part 6

Welcome to Dauntless

You fall into the dark. Then, a net. Hands pull you out. “Welcome to Dauntless.”

Lia is in the net too. She laughs and cries at the same time.

Now you and Lia are initiates: new Dauntless in training. For weeks, you run and you fight. This is stage one. On a big board, there is a red line. If your name is under the line, you are Factionless.

At the end of stage one, your name is over the line. Lia’s name is over the line too, but very close to it.

Tomorrow, stage two begins: your fears. Tonight, the initiates are going to the tattoo shop.

What do you do?

Part 7

The Edge

The others jump. Now only you and Lia are on the roof. And Eric.

“Ten seconds,” says Eric. “Then you are both Factionless.”

Lia’s hands shake. “Go,” she says. “Go without me. Please.”

You look at the dark hole. You look at Lia. In ten seconds, everything changes.

“Five,” says Eric.

What do you do?

Part 8

Tori’s Shop

The tattoo shop is small and loud. Tori works there. She takes you to a back room.

“I remember you,” she says quietly. “Three results.” Then she tells you about her brother. “He was like you. In stage two, he was very fast. A Dauntless leader came to watch him. The next morning, he was dead.”

You are very afraid.

“Listen,” says Tori. “In a simulation, a Divergent thinks: it isn’t real. So a Divergent jumps, or breaks things with one hand. A Dauntless doesn’t do that. A Dauntless is afraid, and finds a way.”

Later, Lia finds you. She is very afraid of stage two. “Some initiates leave before the fear tests,” she whispers. “Let’s leave tonight. Together.”

What do you do?

Part 9

The Glass Box

The next morning, stage two begins. You sit in a chair, and a Dauntless instructor puts a needle in your neck. “The fear ends when you are calm,” she says.

Today, Eric is here too. He watches your fears on a big screen. Next to him, there is a man in blue: Erudite.

Suddenly, you are in a glass box. Water comes in. It goes up to your legs, your chest, your neck. You are very afraid.

You touch the glass. It’s thin. You know this isn’t real. With one hand, you can break it. Easy.

Eric moves closer to the screen.

What do you do?

Part 10

The Final Test

In stage two, you face your fears in a simulation every day, and you learn to stay calm. Then it’s the day of the final test.

Behind a window, the Dauntless leaders watch your fears on a screen. Eric is there. Next to him, there is a man in blue: Erudite.

The needle goes in. You are on the roof of the tallest building in the city. The wind is very strong. The next building is far away. Between the two buildings, there is an old rope.

You are very afraid of heights. Your legs shake.

A voice in your head says: “This isn’t real. Just jump.”

What do you do?

Part 11

The White Room

The man in blue knows everything now. So Erudite knows your secret.

That night, two men in blue come for you. They don’t say a word. Nobody can stop them. They put a needle in your arm, and everything goes black.

You wake up in a white room, on a white bed. You can’t move your hands.

A woman in blue sits next to you. “My name is Jeanine Matthews,” she says. “A Divergent from Candor. Very interesting.”

She opens a small box. Inside, there are many needles.

“Don’t be afraid,” she says, and she smiles. “We only want to look inside your head.”

    Years go by. Every day, you tell the truth about everything. About everything, except one thing.

    At night, from your window, you hear the Dauntless train. It goes past, fast and loud.

          Part 15

          Not Alone

          You hold the rope. You don’t look down. Slowly, very slowly, you cross to the other building. Then the fear ends.

          Eric looks at the screen. “Normal,” he says. “Next.”

          That night, on the roof, Lia takes your hand, like on the first day.

          “On the roof, you didn’t leave me,” she whispers. “At Tori’s shop, you said: stay. So I stayed.”

          She looks around. Nobody is listening.

          “I have a secret,” she says. “My test had three results. Candor, Dauntless, Amity. I’m Divergent.”

          For a long time, you don’t say anything. Then you smile.

          “Me too.”

          For the first time, you are not alone.
