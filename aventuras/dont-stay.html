Don’t Stay

Read each part. At the end, choose 1 or 2. There are three endings. Can you find the good one?

Your path1

Part 1

The Message

You are a new pilot on Cooper Station, the big space station near Saturn.

A message arrives from Dr. Amelia Brand. She lives on a new planet, on the other side of the wormhole.

“A pilot from your station, Leo Marsh, was bringing seeds to my planet. His ship had a problem, and now it is on Miller’s planet. His signal says he is OK.

“I was on that planet once. Time is very slow there. One hour on the planet is seven years at home. And the mountains there are not mountains. They are waves.

“Go and get Leo. Don’t stay.”

Before you leave, Leo’s daughter, Emma, comes to you. She is eight. She gives you a drawing: a man and a girl, hand in hand.

“For my dad,” she says.

Then you fly through the wormhole to Miller’s planet.

What do you do?

Part 2

The Water

Your small ship lands on the water, near Leo’s ship. You step out.

The water is not deep. It comes to your knees. Your body is heavy here. In the sky, you can see Gargantua, the black hole. It is enormous. Far away, there are big gray mountains.

You look at your watch. Every minute here is more than a month at home.

“Leo? Can you hear me?” you say on the radio.

Nobody answers. Leo’s ship is very quiet, and half of it is under the water. You are afraid. Maybe Leo is dead.

What do you do?

Part 3

The Signal

You stay far above the planet. Up here, time is normal. You play Leo’s signal.

Incoming signal

This is Leo Marsh. My ship is on the water planet.

The engine is broken, but I am OK. I have the seeds.

Please tell my daughter, Emma: Dad is coming home for her birthday.

And tell her it’s beautiful here. There are big mountains near my ship.

The message ends. Then it starts again: the same words, the same voice, again and again.

You look down at the blue planet. Where do you land? Near Leo’s ship, on the water? Or on the mountains, high and far from the water?

What do you do?

Part 4

Leo’s Ship

Now you are next to Leo’s ship. It is small, and half of it is under the water. The door is closed, but there is a small window in it.

You look in. A man is sitting inside. He is talking to a small camera, and he is smiling. It’s Leo. He is alive!

You are happy, but you are worried too. Every minute here is more than a month at home. And Leo’s ship can’t fly.

On your radio, you can call Dr. Brand. She knows this planet.

What do you do?

Part 5

The Mountains

You fly down to the mountains. They are big and gray. But something is strange. They move.

They are not mountains. They are waves! One of them is very close, and it is very, very tall.

You turn your ship, fast. The wave passes under you. You are very afraid, and your hands shake. Brand was right.

You look at your watch: ten minutes near the planet. At home, that is more than a year.

Far away, Leo’s ship is still on the water. The wave goes the other way.

What do you do?

Part 6

Leo

You open the door. Leo turns to you. He is about thirty years old, and he has a big smile.

“You’re here already?” he says. “I sent my signal only a few minutes ago!”

For Leo, it was a few minutes. For you and for Emma, it was months.

“I’m making a video for Emma,” he says. “For her birthday. It’s next week.”

You don’t say anything. Emma’s birthday is already over. You feel very sad.

Leo points to some boxes. “These are the seeds. Brand needs them. There are four boxes. Help me, and then we can go.”

The boxes are big and heavy.

What do you do?

Part 7

Dr. Brand

You call Dr. Brand. She answers.

“I was on that planet with my friends,” she says. “I wanted Miller’s data, so we stayed too long. A wave came, and it took our friend Doyle. When we went back up, twenty-three years were gone.

“Listen. The waves come again and again. Leo is going to want the seeds, but people are more important than seeds. A friend told me once: to go forward, you have to leave something behind.”

“Can you send help?” you ask.

“No. There is no time. It’s your decision.”

Then the call ends.

What do you do?

Part 8

The Boxes

You take a box. It is very heavy, and every step in the water is slow.

You carry one box to your ship. Then another. Leo smiles. “Two more,” he says.

You look at your watch. Every box takes many minutes. And every minute here is more than a month for Emma.

You think about Emma at the station. She is waiting for her dad, and she is getting older, month by month.

What do you do?

Part 9

The Drawing

You give Leo Emma’s drawing. He looks at it for a long time: a man and a girl, hand in hand.

“Leo,” you say. “For you, it’s only minutes. For Emma, it’s months. She is waiting for you.”

Leo understands. He is very sad. “Months,” he says quietly.

Then he picks up a box. “OK. We go now. But this box comes with us. It’s only one minute.”

You look behind him. Far away, the gray mountains look bigger now.

What do you do?

Part 10

The Seeds

You carry the seeds with Leo. Then the water around your legs moves, fast. You look up.

The mountain is not a mountain. It’s a wave, and it’s very close.

You and Leo run. You close the door of your ship, and the wave hits. The ship goes up, up, up, and then down again.

You are alive. But the engines are full of water. You wait. One hour. Two hours. Three.

When you arrive at Cooper Station, more than twenty years are gone. A woman is waiting at the door. She is almost thirty. Now she is almost the same age as her father. It’s Emma.

“Hello, Dad,” she says.

Leo looks at her. He is happy and very sad at the same time.

The seeds are safe. But Leo lost twenty years with his daughter.

    Part 11

    The Empty Water

    Your ship goes up, away from the water. You look down.

    A big wave moves across the planet. It covers Leo’s ship. Then the water is calm again, and Leo’s ship is not there.

    You are alive. But you didn’t help Leo.

    You go home to Cooper Station. Emma is waiting at the door. She is taller now.

    “Where is my dad?” she asks.

    You can’t answer. You give her the drawing. She holds it and says nothing.

    After that day, Emma waits at the door every evening.

      Part 12

      Emma

      You take the box from Leo’s hands and drop it in the water.

      “No!” Leo says. “Brand needs the seeds!”

      “Look!” you say. “That mountain is a wave! Emma needs you, Leo, not the seeds.”

      Leo looks at the wave. He looks at the drawing in his hand. Then he runs with you.

      You close the door and start the engines. The wave is very close now. Your ship goes up, over the wave, and away from the water.

      You look at your watch. You were on the planet for about one hour. At home, about seven years are gone.

      At Cooper Station, a girl is waiting at the door. She is fifteen now. Leo can’t speak. He gives her the drawing, and she hugs her father. They both cry.

      A message arrives from Dr. Brand: “We can find more seeds. Good work, pilot.”
